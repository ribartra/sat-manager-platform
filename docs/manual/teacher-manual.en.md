# Teacher manual — SAT Practice Platform

This manual explains how to use the platform as a **teacher**: invite students, assign practice and Tests, follow their results, share reports with parents and manage content. The last part is for the **master teacher**.

> Button and menu names appear **in bold, exactly as shown on screen** (for example **Assign**).

## 1. Getting started

**Activate your account.** The master teacher invites you and you receive an email with a link (valid for 7 days). Open it, choose a password (at least 8 characters), repeat it and click **Finish setup**.

![Teacher account activation](img/teacher-activation.png)

**Sign in.** Type your **Email** and **Password** and click **Log In**. If you forgot it, use **Forgot your password?**.

![Sign in](img/common-login.png)

**The menu.** On the left: **Home**, **Students**, **Tests**, **Practice** and **History** (the master teacher also sees **Teachers**). At the bottom: **Shortcuts**, **Report a problem**, **Notifications** and your name (account menu).

![Side menu](img/common-sidebar.png)

> Each teacher **only sees their own students** (the ones they invited). The question bank, sets and Tests are shared by the whole program.

## 2. Home

Home sums up your work in cards: **Students** (active this week, inactive 5+ days, with Tests to take, invitations), **Tests** and **Practice** (not started, in progress, overdue, completed) and **History** (tries in the last 7 days). Every number opens the filtered list. A set the student already finished counts as **completed** even while they repeat it.

![Home at the start](img/teacher-home-empty.png)

## 3. Students: your students and invitations

### Inviting a student

1. Go to **Students** and click **Invite student**.
2. Fill in **Name**, **Email**, **Phone** (optional), **Start date** (optional) and **Birth date** (required; if they are under 18, at activation they confirm that their parent or guardian agrees).
3. Optional: under **Parent / guardian** type the parent's name, email and phone. Their account is linked when the student activates theirs.
4. Click **Create Invite**.

![Inviting a student](img/teacher-invite-form.png)

The student gets an email with their activation link (valid for 7 days). The screen also shows the link (**Student activation link**) in case you need to send it another way.

![Invitation created](img/teacher-invite-created.png)

> While an invitation is open, the same email can't be invited again.

### The student list

**Students** shows your students with their latest activity and a shortcut per area (**Math**, **Reading**, **Writing**, **Tests**). Filter by name or by status (active this week, inactive 5+ days, with Tests to take). Every row has **Assign**. The **Pending invitations** are listed below.

![Students with no students yet](img/teacher-students-empty.png)

![Pending invitations](img/teacher-students-invitations.png)

![Student list](img/teacher-students.png)

## 4. A student's page

Clicking a student opens their page with tabs:

- **Summary**: the latest try in each area, their Tests and recent activity.
- **Math**, **Reading**, **Writing**: results **by skill and difficulty** (weakest first), their **active sets** (with dates and an **Edit** link to change them), comments and their tries per set.
- **Tests**: scores (total, Reading & Writing, Math), their Tests and tries.

![Student summary](img/teacher-student-summary.png)

![Student's Math results](img/teacher-student-math.png)

![Student's Tests](img/teacher-student-tests.png)

Buttons on the page:

- **Assign practice** / **Assign test**: open the Assign view (see section 5).
- **History**: that student's history.
- **Generate Report**: the PDF report (section 8).
- **Student view**: see the platform **as the student sees it**, read only. Leave with **Exit Student View**.
- **Edit**: change name, email, phone or start date. **Deactivate / Activate**: block or restore their access.
- If the parent's email is missing you'll see **Parent email: missing** and the **Add parent email** button.

![Student view](img/teacher-student-view.png)

## 5. Assigning practice and Tests

The **Assign** view is the same for practice and Tests. Open it from **Assign** in the student list, from the student's page or from a set or Test.

### Assigning practice sets

1. Choose the area in the tabs (**Math**, **Reading**, **Writing**).
2. Open a skill: you'll see one row per active **source** (today, College Board) with one button per level (**Easy**, **Medium**, **Hard**). Click the sets you want to assign. A level with no sets shows **—**.
3. In the summary on the right set:
   - **Open date (optional)**: from when they can start (today by default).
   - **Due date**: the deadline (one week by default).
   - **When to show answers**: **Show answers at the end** or **Show the answer after each question** (the student clicks **Check** on each question).
   - **Show explanations in the player**: shows the explanation when checking (only with answers after each question).
4. Click **Assign (n)**.

![Assign view for practice](img/teacher-assign-practice-empty.png)

![Chosen sets and options](img/teacher-assign-practice-selected.png)

![Assignment options](img/teacher-assign-options.png)

![Assignment done](img/teacher-assign-done.png)

> **Automatic level progression:** when the student passes a level, the platform may assign the next one by itself (for example Medium after Easy), with no due date. You'll see it in their active sets and in **Pending**.

### What is already assigned

- A set the student already has shows **grayed out and can't be picked again** (the student can retry it any time).
- When **every source** of a level is assigned, the level shows the **ALREADY ASSIGNED** stamp. If every source and level of a skill is assigned, the skill stays fixed and doesn't expand.

![Already assigned: grayed and stamped](img/teacher-assign-already-assigned.png)

### Assigning a Test

1. **Tests** tab: each **TEST N** expands into its versions, one per active source (today, Innovator). Each version is named after its source, e.g. **Innovator Test 1**.
2. Tick the version, check the date and options and click **Assign test**.

A TEST N with every version assigned shows as a fixed row with the **ALREADY ASSIGNED** stamp.

![Assigning a Test](img/teacher-assign-test-selected.png)

### Changing an assignment's dates

From the student's page, in their active sets, click **Edit** (or open a row in **Pending**). Change **Open date** or **Due date** and click **Save changes**.

![Editing an assignment's dates](img/teacher-assign-edit.png)

## 6. Pending and History

**Pending** lists everything assigned to your students and not finished yet: overdue first. Filter by student, type, status and period (**All time**, **Last 30 days**, **Last 60 days**, **Last 90 days** or **Custom**, with a start and end date). Click a row to edit its dates.

![Pending](img/teacher-pending.png)

**History** lists every try (practice and Tests), newest first. Filter by student, type, skill, difficulty, status (**Completed**, **In progress**, **Not counted**, **Any**) and period (**All time**, **Last 30 days**, **Last 60 days**, **Last 90 days** or **Custom**, with a start and end date). Practice shows the % correct; Tests show SAT scores. Click a row to open the try.

![History](img/teacher-history.png)

## 7. Reviewing a try and invalidating it

A try's page shows the score, the comparison with the previous try, one square per question and every answer with the right one and the explanation. **Previous** / **Next** move between tries.

![Practice try](img/teacher-attempt.png)

![Test try](img/teacher-test-attempt.png)

**Invalidating a try** (for example, if the student had a connection problem):

1. Click **Invalidate attempt**.
2. Choose the reason (**Health**, **Connection problem** or **Other**).
3. Click **Invalidate attempt**.

The try stays in the history crossed out as **Not counted** and doesn't count in scores or reports. The student can start a new try.

![Invalidating a try](img/teacher-invalidate-dialog.png)

![Invalidated try](img/teacher-attempt-invalidated.png)

## 8. Reports and parents

### PDF report

1. On the student's page click **Generate Report**.
2. Choose the **Period**: **All time**, **Last 30 days**, **Last 60 days**, **Last 90 days** or **Custom** (start and end date). The PDF includes only the tries submitted in that period; pending work always shows. The period is printed at the top of the PDF.
3. Tick the areas (**Math**, **Reading**, **Writing**, **Tests**) and check the preview.
4. Click **Download PDF**, or under **Email to parents** tick the parents and click **Send PDF**.

![PDF report](img/teacher-report-dialog.png)

### Parents

**Parents** (from **Students → Parents**) lists the parents and their students, and flags students with no parent on file. You can:

- **Add Parent**: create a parent and link students.
- **Edit**: change their details.
- **Reports**: share progress by email with a **temporary link** (the parent opens it without a password) or download the PDF.

![Parents](img/teacher-parents.png)

![Sharing progress with a parent](img/teacher-parents-share.png)

## 9. Tests

**Tests** groups the full SATs by number (**TEST N**) with one version per source. Each version is named after its source (**Innovator Test 1**). Every number from TEST 1 to the highest is listed: a number no source has shows as **TEST N —** (in the picture, **TEST 9**). Each Test has 4 modules (R&W 1 and 2, Math 1 and 2). **How scores work** explains how scores are calculated.

![Tests](img/teacher-tests.png)

A Test's page shows its modules, the **Students** table (status, correct answers per module and scores of your students, with **Assign** per row) and the questions of each module. **Options** offers **Preview** (see it as the student), **Assign**, **Edit** and status changes.

![A Test's page](img/teacher-test-page.png)

## 10. Practice and the question bank

**Practice** shows the skill map per area: each skill with its **Easy / Medium / Hard** levels and how many times it was assigned. A level with no set in any source shows as **Hard —**. Opening a skill shows one row per source with its own levels.

![Practice](img/teacher-practice.png)

![A skill opened: the Hard level with no sets (—) and the College Board row](img/teacher-practice-sources.png)

Clicking a level opens its menu: how many students have it assigned, completed, in progress or overdue, and the actions **Open**, **Preview**, **Assign**, **Edit**, **Status** and **Delete** (only if it was never assigned; otherwise archive it).

![A set's menu](img/teacher-practice-menu.png)

A set's page shows how it's going with your students and its questions. **Assign this set** assigns it; **Options** has the same actions.

![A set's page](img/teacher-practice-set.png)

**Questions →** opens the **Question Bank**: every question by area, with filters by domain, skill, difficulty and status.

![Question bank](img/teacher-questions.png)

### Sources

Every set and Test comes from a **source** (where the content came from). Today Practice Sets come from **College Board** and Tests from **Innovator**.

- Only **active** sources are shown: in **Practice**, **Tests**, the filters and **Assign**.
- If an administrator **deactivates** a source, its sets and Tests are no longer shown and can't be assigned. What your students already had assigned keeps working: they can finish it and see their results, and you see it on their page and in **History**.
- Sources are managed by an administrator in **Admin › Sources** (see the AI import and maintenance guide).

> Creating, importing and maintaining sets, Tests and questions is covered in the **AI import and maintenance guide** (Spanish).

The master teacher also has the **Teachers** menu and can import content with AI.

![Master teacher's Home](img/master-home.png)

### Inviting and managing teachers

1. Go to **Teachers** and click **Invite teacher**.
2. Type **Name** and **Email** and click **Send invitation**. The teacher receives a link to activate their account.

![Teachers](img/master-teachers-list.png)

![Inviting a teacher](img/master-teacher-invite-form.png)

Open invitations show under **Pending invitations**, with **Cancel invitation**. Each teacher can be **deactivated** (**Deactivate**) or reactivated; opening one shows their details and students.

![Pending invitation](img/master-teachers-pending.png)

![A teacher's page](img/master-teacher-detail.png)

### Importing content with AI

In **Practice** and **Tests** the master teacher sees **Import with AI** while something is still missing (when everything is complete, the button is not shown), and the gaps can be imported: in **Practice**, when a skill is opened, a source's empty level shows **+ Hard** (or another level; the skill row shows **Hard —**); in **Tests**, a missing number appears as **TEST N —** and each source offers **+ Import TEST N**. The whole process is in the **AI import and maintenance guide**.

## 13. Frequently asked questions

**A student didn't get the invitation.**
Check their email and spam folder. You can copy the **Student activation link** and send it to them. If 7 days have passed, invite them again.

**Can I assign the same set twice?**
There's no need: the student can repeat it any time with **Try Again**. The set stays **Completed** while they repeat it (you'll see **Retry in progress**) and never turns overdue. To change dates use **Edit**.

**Why can't I see some students?**
Each teacher only sees the students they invited.

**What happens if I invalidate a try?**
It stays visible but crossed out (**Not counted**) and stops counting in scores and reports.

**I no longer see a source's sets or Tests.**
An administrator deactivated it. What was already assigned is still on each student's page and in **History**. To see them again, ask for it to be reactivated in **Admin › Sources**.

**Does a parent need a password to see progress?**
Not if you share a link from **Parents → Reports**: they open it without a password for a few days.
