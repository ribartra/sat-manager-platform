# Student manual — SAT Practice Platform

This manual explains how to use the platform as a **student**: activate your account, practice by skill, take full SAT-style Tests and review your results.

> Button and menu names appear **in bold, exactly as shown on screen** (for example **Start practice**).

## 1. Activate your account

There is no open sign-up: **your teacher invites you** and you receive an email with an activation link. The link is valid for **7 days**.

1. Open the link in the email. You'll see your name, email, phone and program (they can't be changed here).
2. Type a password in **Password** and repeat it in **Type the password again**. It must have **at least 8 characters** and can't be the part of your email before the @.
3. Read the Terms of Use and the Privacy Policy. The **I have read and accept…** box is ticked; if you are under 18, your parent or guardian must agree.
4. Click **Finish setup**.

![Student account activation](img/student-activation.png)

You then reach the sign-in page with a notice that your account is ready.

![Account activated, ready to sign in](img/student-login-activated.png)

> If the link says it no longer works or has expired, ask your teacher for a new invitation.

## 2. Sign in and password

1. Open the platform and type your **Email** and **Password**.
2. Click **Log In**.

![Sign in](img/common-login.png)

**Forgot your password?** Click **Forgot your password?**, type your email and click **Send reset link**. You'll receive a link (valid for 1 hour) to choose a new password.

![Password recovery](img/common-forgot-password.png)

> For security, you are signed out after **10 minutes without activity**. Your answers are kept: sign in again and continue where you left off.

## 3. The Home page

The left menu has **Home**, **Tests**, **Practice** and **History**. At the bottom are **Report a problem**, **Notifications** and your name (account menu).

Home sums everything up in three cards:

- **Tests**: how many Tests you have to take and their status (**Not started**, **In progress**, **Overdue**, **Completed**), plus the **Up next** list.
- **Practice**: practice sets to do, by area (**Math**, **Reading**, **Writing**) and by status, including **Completed**.
- **History**: what you finished in the last 7 days and your latest tries with their percentage.

Every number is a link that opens the list already filtered.

![Home right after activation](img/student-home.png)

![Home with activity](img/student-home-active.png)

If something is **overdue**, it shows in the **Overdue** counter:

![Home with an overdue set](img/student-home-overdue.png)

## 4. Practice: practicing by skill

**Practice** shows your SAT skills by area. Choose the **Math**, **Reading** or **Writing** tab.

- The main section shows your results **by skill and difficulty** (**Easy**, **Medium**, **Hard**) and points out your weakest skill.
- On the right, **Your practice sets** lists what your teacher assigned, with its status, when it opens and when it's due. Click **Start** or **Continue**.
- Further down are your tries per set (the last 3 of each).

![Practice before practicing](img/student-practice-new.png)

![Practice with results](img/student-practice.png)

Opening a set shows its card: area, difficulty, source, number of questions, due date and your previous tries. Click **Start practice** (or **Start try 2**, **Continue where you left off**).

![A practice set's card](img/student-practice-intro.png)

![A set's card after one try](img/student-practice-intro-retry.png)

### Classroom and Homework

Your teacher assigns each set as **Classroom** (work in class) or **Homework**; every set carries its tag.

- **Classroom** has no score: you see what you answered ("k of N answered"), no percentage.
- **Homework** is scored (percentage) and it is what counts in your results.
- If a set you started in class is assigned to you as Homework, the class work is closed and the Homework starts from zero.

At the top, **All · Classroom · Homework** filters what you see.

![Practice in Classroom](img/student-practice-context.png)

![Result of a class set, no score](img/student-classroom-result.png)

## 5. Working on a practice set

The player uses the whole screen:

- **Top**: the set name, the question number, the timer (hide it with **Hide**) and the tools.
- **Left**: the question list (**Questions**), with filters by difficulty and by status (unanswered, answered, marked).
- **Center**: the text or passage and the question.
- **Right**: the answer choices.
- **Bottom**: **Back**, **Next** and the **Question N of M** navigator.

![Practice player](img/player-question.png)

### Answering

- Click a choice (A, B, C or D), or type your answer in **Type your answer** for numeric questions (fractions like 1/2 or decimals like 0.5 work).
- Your answers **are saved automatically** ("Your answers are saved automatically").

![Answer selected](img/player-answer-selected.png)

### Checking each answer (if your teacher turned it on)

If your teacher chose to show the answer after each question, a **Check** button appears:

1. Choose your answer and click **Check**.
2. You'll see **Correct** or **Incorrect** and the right answer. If your teacher turned explanations on, you'll also see the **Explanation**.
3. A checked question can't be changed.

![Correct answer with explanation](img/player-check-feedback.png)

![Incorrect answer](img/player-check-incorrect.png)

### Tools

- **Highlight**: select text in the passage or question and click Highlight.
- **Eliminate**: turns on a mode to cross out choices you rule out.
- **Reference** (Math only): opens the official formula sheet (**Open full size** shows it larger).
- **Calculator** (Math only, when the administrator enabled it).
- **Mark for review**: flags the question so you can come back.
- **Personal note**: a note just for you.
- **Report**: tells us about a problem with the question.
- **Save & exit**: stops the timer and leaves; when you come back you continue where you left off.

![Eliminate mode on](img/player-eliminate.png)

![Question marked for review, with a personal note](img/player-mark-note.png)

![Math reference sheet](img/player-reference.png)

**Keyboard shortcuts**: ← and → to go back or forward, A–D (or 1–4) to choose, **M** to mark.

### Review and submit

1. On the last question click **Review**. The **Check your work** page shows every question: answered, left blank and marked. Click a number to go back to it.
2. You can also open the **Question N of M** navigator at any time.
3. Click **Submit** and confirm.

![Check your work page](img/player-review.png)

![Question navigator](img/player-navigator.png)

![Confirm submission](img/player-submit-confirm.png)

If you left questions blank, the notice reminds you: **blank questions count as wrong**.

![Blank questions notice](img/player-submit-confirm-blank.png)

### Leave and continue later

**Save & exit** leaves without submitting. The set stays **In progress** and you continue on the same question when you come back.

![Back after Save & exit](img/student-after-save-exit.png)

> **Offline**: if the internet drops, keep working. You'll see a notice and your answers upload when the connection is back.

## 6. Your results

After submitting you see your results:

- The **percentage**, how many you got right, wrong or left blank, and the comparison with your previous try (for example "▲ 77% vs. try 1").
- **Your answers at a glance**: one square per question (green right, red wrong). Click one to see it.
- **Your answers, question by question**: your answer, the right one and the **Explanation**, when your teacher allows it.
- Buttons: **Try Again** (new try), **Next** (suggested next set) and **Summary**.

![A set's results](img/student-results-top.png)

![Full results](img/student-results.png)

> If your teacher hid the answers for a set, you'll see a notice instead of the right answers.

**You can repeat a set as often as you like** with **Try Again**: every try is kept and you can follow your progress. While you repeat it, the set stays **Completed** with the **Retry in progress** mark; click **Continue** to go on with that try.

> When you pass a level, the platform may **open the next one for you automatically** (for example Medium after Easy).

## 7. Tests: the full SAT

**Tests** holds the full SATs your teacher assigned. Each Test has **4 modules**: **Reading & Writing 1 and 2** and **Math 1 and 2**. The tabs are **To take**, **Completed** and **All**. Each Test is named after its source, e.g. **Innovator Test 1**.

![Tests list](img/student-tests.png)

### Starting a module

1. Open the Test and choose a module. **You can take them in any order.**
2. Read the **Directions** and click **Start module** (or **Continue module**).
3. The timer counts down; if you leave, **it keeps your remaining time**.

![A module's card](img/student-test-intro.png)

![Test player](img/test-player.png)

The Test player works like the practice player (Highlight, Eliminate, Mark for review, note, Save & exit). You can only go back to questions **in the same module**.

> **Extra time**: if time runs out, you can keep working. You'll see **EXTRA TIME** and your teacher will see how much extra time you used.

### Submitting the module

1. On the last question click **Review** and check **Check your work**.
2. Click **Submit module** and confirm. **A submitted module can't be changed.**

![Module review](img/test-review.png)

![Confirm module submission](img/test-submit-module.png)

You'll see the module's results and what's left. When all 4 are done you'll see your **total score (out of 1600)** and per section.

![Module results](img/test-results-module.png)

![Tests with one module completed](img/student-tests-after.png)

When you finish a Test you can take it again with **Retake**.

![Pending](img/student-pending.png)

![Pending with an overdue set](img/student-pending-overdue.png)

**History** shows everything you did, newest first, with your score and the change from the previous try. Filter by type, skill, difficulty, status and period (**All time**, **Last 30 days**, **Last 60 days**, **Last 90 days** or **Custom**, with a start and end date). Click a row to open that try.

## 10. Frequently asked questions

**Do I lose my answers if the internet drops or I close the tab?**
No. They save automatically and upload when the connection is back.

**Can I change an answer I already checked, or a submitted module?**
No. Checked questions and submitted modules are final.

**Why can't I see the right answers for a set?**
Your teacher decides whether they're shown. Ask them.

**Can a try not count?**
Yes. If your teacher invalidates it (for example, because of a connection problem), it stays in your history crossed out as **Not counted**. You can start a new try.

**I didn't get the activation email.**
Check your spam folder. If 7 days have passed, ask your teacher for a new invitation.
